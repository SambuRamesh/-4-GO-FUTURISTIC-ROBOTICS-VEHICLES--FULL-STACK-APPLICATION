PRODUCT DETAILS

Bujji is a cutting-edge electric vehicle designed with advanced AI technology for enhanced performance and safety. It features a powerful 94 kW motor that delivers high torque for smooth acceleration and efficient energy use. Equipped with a 47 kWh battery, Bujji offers a long driving range suitable for urban and off-road conditions. The car includes smart sensors and autonomous driving capabilities, enabling it to navigate complex environments with minimal human intervention. Its sleek design incorporates lightweight materials for improved efficiency, and the vehicle supports fast charging for reduced downtime. Overall, Bujji combines innovation, power, and sustainability in a futuristic package.

PRODUCT DETAILS

Bujji stands out with its state-of-the-art AI-driven navigation system that ensures precise and safe driving even in challenging terrains. It is equipped with advanced safety features like collision detection, automatic emergency braking, and lane-keeping assist to protect passengers and pedestrians. The vehicle’s smart connectivity allows seamless integration with mobile devices, providing real-time updates on battery health, route planning, and vehicle diagnostics. Additionally, Bujji’s ergonomic interior design focuses on comfort and user experience, featuring customizable digital displays and voice-controlled commands. These innovative features make Bujji a smart, efficient, and eco-friendly choice for the future of transportation.
